# A little room to think.

Your notes, connected. Your files, always yours.

This is a working notebook built in Rust. Open a note from the sidebar, follow a link, or press **Ctrl+E** to start writing.

## Make yourself at home

- **Write freely.** Plain Markdown, saved directly to your vault.
- **Connect ideas.** Use [[Notes/Connected thinking|wiki links]] to move between notes.
- **Find your way.** Press **Ctrl+O** to jump to any note.
- **Keep the familiar.** Files on the left, your writing in the middle, context on the right.

## A calmer workspace

> The best tool leaves enough room for the thought.

The charcoal palette and teal accents come from your existing Obsidian theme. This notebook keeps that familiar shape while its interface and editor run natively in Rust.

Start with [[Notes/First steps]], or take a look at the [[Projects/Native notebook|project notebook]].
