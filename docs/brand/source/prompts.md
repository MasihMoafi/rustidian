# Rustidian original logo prompts (3 Nov). Goal: an original mark that shares no outline, facets or construction with Obsidian's faceted gem. Smooth rounded stone forms only; no straight-edged crystal planes.

Shared tail (every prompt): This is a square app and toolbar icon, unmistakable at 24 px, no detail smaller than one eighth of its width, filling 85% of the square canvas. Genuine transparent background. Flat fills in four reds only: #55000E, #8B0A1E, #D12B3A, #FF5A5E. Generate only this one isolated mark. No gradients, glow, outlines, text, badge, mockup, crystal, gem, facets, sharp points.

## 1. River pebble
Design ONE original Rustidian desktop app logo: a single smooth river pebble resting on a flat base, wider than tall (about 5:4), carrying the meaning of a solid, durable home for notes. The silhouette is a soft rounded oval, slightly lopsided, with a gently flattened bottom. Inside it, three thick curved bands run across the pebble like layers of sediment, each band a different red, darkest at the bottom, lit from the upper left; the bands follow the pebble's roundness and are separated by thin seams of #FFB3AD.

## 2. Cairn
Design ONE original Rustidian desktop app logo: a cairn of three smooth stacked stones, carrying the meaning of notes stacked and connected. A large wide flat-bottomed rounded stone at the base, a medium rounded stone resting on it slightly to the left, a small rounded stone on top slightly to the right. Every stone is a soft rounded blob with a different red body and a small lighter highlight patch at its upper left; small transparent gaps separate the stones.

## 3. Split stone
Design ONE original Rustidian desktop app logo: one smooth rounded stone cleanly split in two by a single gently curved crack running from upper right to lower left, the right half nudged slightly outward and upward, carrying the meaning of opening your notes. Both halves are soft and rounded, left half in #D12B3A with a #FF5A5E highlight patch at the upper left, right half in #8B0A1E with a #55000E shadow patch at the lower right; the crack is a transparent gap about one twentieth of the stone's width.

## Refinement of 1 (4 and 5): the pebble was too smooth and jelly-like; the layers are kept, the silhouette gets a natural, worn, irregular outline.
Shared tail as above.

## 4. Worn stone
Design ONE original Rustidian desktop app logo: a single worn natural stone resting on a flat base, wider than tall (about 4:3), carrying the meaning of a solid, durable home for notes. The silhouette is an irregular rounded stone: higher and rounder on the left, a long gentle slope down to the right, a flattened bottom, and two small rounded chips bitten out of the outline, one on the upper right and one on the lower left. Inside it, three uneven curved strata bands of different thickness run across the stone, darkest at the bottom, lit from the upper left, separated by thin seams of #FFB3AD that follow the stone's roundness. The surface is matte and flat, with no shine and no highlight blob.

## 5. Quarried stone
Design ONE original Rustidian desktop app logo: a single chunky natural stone resting on a flat base, wider than tall (about 4:3), carrying the meaning of a solid, durable home for notes. The silhouette is an irregular, lumpy, rounded boulder with three soft bulges along its top and a flat bottom, as if smoothed by water. Two thick curved strata bands cut across it at different angles, the lower band darker, separated by thin seams of #FFB3AD; one small round pit sits in the upper band. The surface is matte and flat, no shine and no highlight blob.
